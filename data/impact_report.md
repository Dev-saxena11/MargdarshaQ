# Real-world impact of quantum-inspired route optimisation

_Generated by `scripts/impact_report.py` on 2026-09-14 against synthetic city networks. Re-run it to reproduce every figure below._

Route optimisation is usually reported as a percentage against another algorithm, which tells you nothing about whether it matters. This converts the same results into fuel, emissions and driver time.

## The headline

On the largest instance measured (40 deliveries, 9 vehicles), replacing ordinary nearest-stop dispatch with our optimiser changes one day's operation by:

- **+5.8 hours** of driver time
- **-13.6 litres** of diesel
- **-36.4 kg** of CO2

Per 100 deliveries that is **-34.0 litres** of fuel and **-91.0 kg** of CO2.

**Read that carefully: this is a trade, not a clean win.** The optimiser drives 108.6 km further and burns 13.6 litres more fuel, in exchange for 5.8 hours of driver time. Whether that is worth it depends on what an operator values — driver wages and delivery promises, or fuel and emissions. We report both rather than the flattering one.

On smaller instances the same optimiser does save distance (20 deliveries below), so the trade-off appears as the problem grows rather than being inherent to the method.

## Assumptions

- **8 km per litre** — diesel light commercial vehicle on an urban duty cycle. Highway figures for this class are 10-14 km/L; stop-start delivery work is worse, so this is deliberately conservative.
- **2.68 kg CO2 per litre** — DEFRA/IPCC combustion factor for diesel. Implies 0.335 kg CO2 per km.
- **250 working days per year** for annual figures.

Change any of these in `app/core/impact.py` and re-run; every number here derives from that one place.

## Method

Each row solves the same delivery problem twice: once with greedy nearest-stop dispatch (what ordinary routing software does) and once with the quantum-inspired optimiser. Both are scored on the same network with the same traffic, and both must respect vehicle capacity and customer time windows. The difference between them is what is reported.
